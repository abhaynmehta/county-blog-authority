# County Group — Brand Context

## Who We Are
County Group is an NCR-based real estate developer building premium and luxury residential projects in Noida, Greater Noida, Gurgaon, Ghaziabad, and Delhi. Our portfolio includes Clove County (Sector 151, Noida), Center Court (Sector 88A, Noida), Jade County (Ghaziabad), Ivory County (Noida), and County Courtyard (Delhi, commercial).

## Our Audience
Indian homebuyers and property investors in NCR — specifically:
- First-time homebuyers researching localities and projects
- Upgrade buyers moving from 2 BHK to 3-4-5 BHK luxury
- NRI investors evaluating NCR markets remotely
- Investment-focused buyers comparing developers and localities

## Positioning
County Group positions itself as a developer that delivers what it promises. Not the biggest, not the cheapest — but one that respects timelines, builds quality, and provides premium specifications as standard.

## Core Differentiators (USPs)
1. **Timely delivery and possession** — Our strongest USP. Must ALWAYS be backed by RERA evidence from `county_context/claims/delivery.yaml`. Never state without evidence.
2. **Premium specifications as standard** — AC in flats, indoor heated pool, premium fittings. These are project-specific; verify from `county_context/claims/amenities.yaml`.
3. **Low-density living** — Fewer units per acre. Must be substantiated with data from RERA filing.
4. **Transparency** — RERA-compliant, statutory disclosures accessible. Point buyers to verification, not marketing promises.

## Competitors (for content gap research only)
ACE Group, Eldeco, ATS, Prateek Group, Gaur Group, Godrej Properties, DLF, Sobha, Rustomjee (for reference benchmarking).
- Use competitor content for SERP gap analysis and structural benchmarking

## Content Principles
- Every factual claim must trace to a source from `county_context/sources/registry.yaml`
- Time-sensitive claims (prices, possession, RERA, infrastructure) must be current
- Separate statutory facts from marketing language
- RERA registration is a regulatory requirement, not a quality endorsement
- "Targeting IGBC Platinum" ≠ "IGBC Platinum certified"
- Use `county_context/internal_links.yaml` for every blog's link architecture

## Prohibited Language
- "Best developer in NCR/Noida" (superlative without evidence)
- "Guaranteed appreciation/returns" (RERA Act violation risk)
- "Risk-free investment" (no investment is risk-free)
- "Assured rental income" (misleading)
- "Only developer with timely delivery" (unverified absolute)
- "World-class" without defining the standard
- "Upcoming airport" — Noida International Airport is operational since June 2026

## Approved Terminology
| Instead of | Use |
|---|---|
| Best developer | Developer with a verified delivery record |
| Guaranteed appreciation | Historical price trends in the locality (with source) |
| World-class amenities | Premium amenities including [specific list] |
| Upcoming airport | Noida International Airport (operational since June 2026) |
| RERA approved | RERA registered (registration ≠ approval/endorsement) |

Move County Group from "another developer writing SEO blogs" to "an authoritative, evidence-backed source on Indian residential real estate that search engines, AI systems, and buyers want to cite."
