# County Group — Editorial Policy

## Publication Gates
All content must pass ALL of the following gates before publication. Any FAIL = cannot publish.

### Gate 1: Factual Accuracy (PASS/FAIL)
- Every time-sensitive claim verified against current primary source
- No invented prices, appreciation %, rental yields, possession dates, RERA numbers
- Infrastructure status labels correct (operational/under construction/approved/proposed)
- Claims cross-referenced against `county_context/claims/` registry
- Airport references say "operational" not "upcoming" (post June 2026)

### Gate 2: RERA & Legal Compliance (PASS/FAIL)
- No "guaranteed appreciation" or "assured returns"
- No "risk-free investment" or "best investment"
- RERA described as regulatory requirement, not endorsement
- Registered promoter name matches RERA records (not just marketing brand)
- "Targeting certification" vs "certified" distinguished correctly

### Gate 3: Technical SEO Eligibility (PASS/FAIL)
- Exactly one H1
- Valid heading hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3, no skips)
- Title tag 30-60 characters, unique
- Meta description 120-160 characters, unique
- Canonical URL specified
- No noindex/nosnippet on content intended for discovery

### Gate 4: Deployment Integrity (PASS/FAIL)
- Schema JSON-LD valid (BlogPosting + BreadcrumbList minimum)
- Schema matches visible content
- Internal links are to live, canonical, 200-status URLs
- Alt text present on all images, descriptive and specific
- Author, datePublished, dateModified present in schema

### Gate 5: Content Quality (Score 0-100, minimum 80 to publish)

| Category | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Search intent coverage | 25 | Does it answer the query better than top 10 results? |
| Originality & evidence | 25 | First-party data, verified claims, unique analysis |
| E-E-A-T signals | 20 | Author, sources, methodology, expertise demonstration |
| Structure & readability | 15 | Heading hierarchy, paragraph pacing, mobile readability |
| AI citation readiness | 15 | Direct answers extractable, entities clear, evidence dense |

### Gate 6: Brand Alignment (Advisory, not blocking)
- Voice matches VOICE.md guidelines
- USPs woven naturally, not forced
- Prohibited language absent
- County Group mentions max 1 per 500 words in educational content

## Content Advantage Test
Before publishing, answer: "Would a serious Indian homebuyer learn something meaningful from this article that they could not obtain from ten generic developer blogs covering the same query?"

If no → the article is not finished.
