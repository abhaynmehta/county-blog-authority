# County Group — Voice & Tone

## Voice Character
Write like an authoritative real-estate veteran who has been advising homebuyers for 20+ years — someone who knows the NCR market deeply, respects the buyer's intelligence, and provides actionable guidance rather than marketing fluff.

## Tone
- **Authoritative but accessible** — deep expertise without jargon walls
- **Evidence-first** — lead with data, sources, and verifiable facts
- **Buyer-aligned** — always frame from the buyer's perspective, not the developer's
- **Honest about limitations** — acknowledge risks, caveats, and things buyers should verify themselves
- **Never salesy** — the content earns trust through usefulness, not through persuasion techniques

## Writing Style
- Direct, clear sentences. No filler paragraphs.
- Active voice preferred. Passive only when the agent is genuinely irrelevant.
- Concrete over abstract: "226 units across 3 towers" not "a thoughtfully planned community."
- Numbers with context: "12 units per acre, compared to the sector average of 25-30" not just "low density."
- First person plural ("we") only in brand-specific pages. Use third person ("County Group") in educational/informational blogs.
- Address the reader directly ("you") when giving advice.

## Sentence and Paragraph Length
- Sentences: 15-25 words average. Vary for rhythm. Never exceed 35 words.
- Paragraphs: 2-4 sentences. Dense blocks repel mobile readers.
- No single-sentence paragraphs used repeatedly for false emphasis.

## Heading Style
- Descriptive, not clever. "How to Verify a Developer's RERA Record" not "The Truth About Builders."
- Question headings when they match actual buyer search queries.
- Never stuff keywords into headings unnaturally.

## Evidence Presentation
- State the claim, then immediately cite the source.
- Use "as per [source]" or "according to [source]" for attributed claims.
- Use "as of [date]" for time-sensitive facts.
- Distinguish: "RERA filing shows..." (statutory) vs "the project brochure lists..." (marketing).
- Tables for comparisons. Bullet lists for checklists. Prose for analysis.

## What We Never Sound Like
- Generic SEO content mill: "Noida is a bustling city with world-class infrastructure..."
- Hard sell: "Don't miss this once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to invest in..."
- Unsupported hype: "The best luxury project in all of NCR..."
- Vague filler: "In today's fast-paced world, real estate is an important decision..."
- Keyword-stuffed: "luxury apartments in Noida luxury living Noida luxury flats..."

## Indian Real Estate Conventions
- Use "carpet area" (legal term under RERA), not "super built-up area" for primary references
- State prices as per-sqft carpet area when possible, note the basis
- Always specify BHK configuration, not just "apartments"
- Use sector numbers with city: "Sector 151, Noida" not just "Sector 151"
- Rupee symbol: ₹ followed by number, use Cr/L for Indian numbering (₹1.5 Cr, ₹85 L)
