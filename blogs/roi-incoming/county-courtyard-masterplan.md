# County Courtyard Master Plan

A Development Planned as One Cohesive System. 1,10,347 sq. ft. of retail space and 2,82,875 sq. ft. of office space, supported by 13,000 sq. m. of leisure area.

## Zoning Strategy
- **Lower Levels:** High-visibility retail zones
- **Mid Levels:** Food courts, entertainment, open restaurants
- **Upper Levels:** Office ecosystem with privacy

## Twin Tower Configuration
Wing A and Wing B connected through Central Cultural Plaza.

## Retail Planning
- 91.0 metres of retail frontage in Wing A
- 93.0 metres of retail frontage in Wing B
- Linear, high-visibility retail corridors

## Wing A: Vertical Distribution
- 3-Level Basement (parking)
- Ground & First: Core retail
- Second: Open restaurants
- 3rd-23rd: Office spaces

## Wing B: Layered Experience
- 3-Level Basement (parking)
- Ground & First: Retail
- 2nd-3rd: Entertainment and food courts
- 4th: Open restaurants
- 5th-21st: Office spaces

## Leisure Planning (13,000 sq. m.)
- Central Cultural Plaza
- Amphitheatre for events
- Rooftop Lounge
