# County Courtyard, NSP: An Integrated Business & Lifestyle Hub in Delhi

Elevating the skyline of the region, County Courtyard from County Group is an emerging commercial destination in Netaji Subhash Place, Pitampura.

One of Delhi's most prominent commercial districts. Connected to Delhi Metro Red and Pink Lines. Nearby stations: NSP, Shakurpur, Pitampura.

- Terrace level
