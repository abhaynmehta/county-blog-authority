## Why the Noida Expressway Corridor is at the Centre of This Shift

The development of Noida International Airport (Jewar Airport) is expected to influence growth far beyond the airport itself. One of the areas set to benefit the most is the Noida Expressway corridor, where strong infrastructure, established social amenities, and expanding commercial activity are creating the ideal setting for premium residential growth.

Some of the factors driving this momentum include:

  - **Strategic location:** The corridor enjoys a unique position between the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway, the Yamuna Expressway, and Noida International Airport (Jewar Airport). Together, these infrastructure corridors improve regional accessibility and strengthen links between Noida, Greater Noida, Delhi, and emerging growth centres, making the area increasingly attractive for both residential and commercial development. 
  - **Well-connected transport network:** Connectivity extends well beyond road infrastructure, giving residents multiple ways to travel across the NCR.
      - **By Metro:** The Aqua Line connects several residential and commercial sectors across Noida and Greater Noida, while its planned extension to Botanical Garden will improve interchange with the Delhi Metro's Blue and Magenta Lines.
      - **By Road:** Direct access to Delhi, Greater Noida, and major business districts through the expressway network supports smoother daily commuting and regional travel.

Together, these advantages have transformed the Noida Expressway corridor into one of the city's most preferred destinations for premium residential development.
