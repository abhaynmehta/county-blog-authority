## Where is Sector 88 Located in Gurgaon?

Sector 88 sits in New Gurgaon, the planned extension of the city that has been developed along a well-laid grid of wide roads and organised land use. Unlike the older, denser parts of Gurgaon, New Gurgaon was designed with room to breathe, larger plots, broader arterial roads and dedicated green zones.

The sector's position is one of its strongest assets. It borders Dwarka Expressway (NH-248BB) to the north, which connects directly to Delhi and the IGI Airport corridor. NH-48, the main Delhi-Gurgaon expressway, is accessible within a short drive. Pataudi Road runs along the sector's southern edge, linking it to Manesar and the industrial belt beyond. The Central Peripheral Road (CPR) along with the nearby Southern Peripheral Road (SPR) and the Dwarka Expressway, formerly known as the Northern Peripheral Road, strengthens connectivity to Delhi and other parts of Gurugram and gives residents a congestion-free alternative to the older arterial routes.

Sector 88A, the sub-sector where most of the premium residential development is concentrated, benefits from all of these access points while sitting slightly set back from the main expressway, which means you get the connectivity without the noise and density that comes with living directly on a highway corridor.
