# Location Map

Nestled in one of Noida's fastest emerging residential corridors, Clove County in Sector 151 offers premium flats in Noida with a location that combines strong connectivity, planned development and access to the city's expanding infrastructure.

Surrounded by emerging sectors such as 150, 152 and 153, the project sits along the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway, offering convenient access to Noida, Greater Noida and the wider Delhi-NCR region.

## How to Reach Clove County

### By Road

Clove County is located just off the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway corridor, offering convenient access from Noida, Greater Noida, Delhi, Ghaziabad, Gurugram and Faridabad. From the expressway, visitors can take the connecting sector roads towards Sector 151 to reach the project. The wider road network also provides onward connectivity to the Yamuna Expressway (10 mins) and Eastern Peripheral Expressway.

### By Metro

The Noida Sector 148 Metro Station, part of the Aqua Line, is just 5 mins away and offers convenient access to the area. From the station, Clove County can be reached via a short onward journey by cab or other local transport.
