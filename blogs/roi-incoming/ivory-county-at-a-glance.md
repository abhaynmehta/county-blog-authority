# Experience Elevated Living at Ivory County

Located in Sector 115, Noida, Ivory County will be a landmark of luxury living. Set within a fast-evolving urban corridor, the project redefines what it means to live in a well-designed, premium community of 3BHK, 4BHK and 5BHK apartments.

## Project Highlights

- **28-acre** integrated luxury development
- **11 acres** of landscaped greens and themed outdoor zones
- **4-acre** lagoon serving as the community's leisure centrepiece
- **2,372 premium 3, 4, and 5 BHK residences** planned across the master layout

## Density & Planning Philosophy

Ivory County has been envisioned as a low-density community of premium flats in Noida where residents enjoy openness, privacy, and unhindered movement.

- Wide internal roads for smooth movement
- Thoughtfully spaced towers for ventilation and natural light
- Large green-to-built ratio supporting wellness-focused living
- Zoned planning that separates pedestrian, play, and vehicular areas

## Design & Architectural Identity

Developed by County Group, one of the leading developers in Noida:

- Resort-inspired arrival experience
- Lagoon-centric layout creating a visual and experiential anchor
- Tower orientations optimised for sunlight and privacy
- The Nature Bridge, a unique architectural element offering elevated views
- The Garden of Stillness, crafted as a meditative green retreat

## Sustainability & Green Living

- Landscape-led cooling with green buffers
- Water-efficient planning
- Natural ventilation strategies across common areas
- Energy-conscious lighting design for shared spaces

## Safety & Community Framework

- Gated community environment
- Multi-tier security systems
- Controlled access points
- Well-lit internal pathways and common zones

## The Ivory County Promise

Ivory County comprehends the essence of modern aspirations, including space, serenity, luxury, and accessibility, and transforms them into a living experience that resonates deeply with discerning homeowners.
