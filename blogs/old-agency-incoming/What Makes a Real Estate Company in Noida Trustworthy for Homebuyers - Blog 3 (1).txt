Red Flags That Should Make You Think Twice
It’s important to recognise early warning signs that may indicate a lack of transparency or reliability from a developer:
	•	High-pressure booking tactics – Statements like “prices will increase tomorrow” are often used to rush decisions without proper evaluation.
	•	Repeated project delays without clarity – Occasional delays can happen, but a consistent pattern of 2–3 year overruns without clear justification is a concern.
	•	Lack of product knowledge among sales teams – If basic details like carpet area versus super built-up area are unclear, it reflects poorly on overall professionalism.
	•	Discouraging site visits – Reputable developers are open to site inspections, even during construction phases. Avoiding access can be a red flag.
	•	Unclear possession or amenities timelines – Vague commitments like “coming soon” without defined dates should be treated cautiously.
	•	Unusually low pricing – Offers significantly below market rates often come with trade-offs such as location compromises, construction quality issues, or hidden costs.
